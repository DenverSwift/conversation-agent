# Sales And Support Agent Landscape

Access date: 2026-07-28.

## Summary

Commercial sales/support tools solve routing, CRM workflow, knowledge-base grounding, team inboxes, and handoff better than the current project should attempt in early stages. They do not solve Matvey-specific Telegram style evidence or human-like account behavior as a portable local core.

## Reviewed Services

- Chat Data: hosted Telegram chatbot and Telegram Business chatbot support, including owner takeover and customer replies.
- ChatPlace: no-code bot/CRM platform with Telegram, pricing tiers, CRM integrations, and an AI Agent that can use uploaded knowledge files.
- BotB2B: AI assistant for Telegram bots and Telegram Business DMs with token-based usage and team add-ons.
- respond.io: strongest reviewed commercial workflow platform, with AI agents for lead qualification, CRM updates, routing, product recommendations, omnichannel operations, and RAG over verified knowledge sources.

## Fit For Conversation Agent

Adopt patterns, not a full platform. Buy or integrate CRM/handoff later, keep the early Telegram behavior runtime custom, avoid coupling style memory to SaaS support data models, and validate business workflows after personal-style reliability is proven.
